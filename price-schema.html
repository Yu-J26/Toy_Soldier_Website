Price feed schema

price-feed.json is the canonical current offer feed. prices.csv is its flat export for spreadsheet and CSV scraper use. Keep their offer IDs, quantities, prices, terms, dates, and availability values in sync.

CSV columns

ColumnMeaning
offer_idStable identifier for the offer.
offer_nameHuman-readable offer name.
product_quantitiesJSON object mapping product IDs to quantities in one offer.
total_priceTotal simulated price for one offer.
currencyCurrency label for the simulated price (USD).
eligibility_termsOffer eligibility and purchase terms.
effective_fromISO 8601 timestamp when the offer takes effect.
expires_atISO 8601 timestamp when the offer expires.
simulated_availabilityAvailability source. buyer-ledger-authoritative means the buyer ledger controls virtual stock.
market_modesimulation-only; no physical goods or real payments are involved.

JSON feed

The JSON feed also includes seller metadata, product descriptions and listed unit prices, schema version, feed timestamps, availability rules, and the per-soldier price cap.

Record every price or promotion change in price-history.json and update both current offer feeds together.
